# Nanobody-Antigen Docking Benchmarks

Nanobodies (single-domain antibodies, VHH) bind their antigens mostly through their three CDR loops, the third one (CDR3) being long and variable. Their structures are predicted rather than known, so the docking starts from an ensemble of predicted nanobody models and the unbound antigen.

The dataset contains 40 nanobody-antigen complexes, from M. Sánchez-Marín, M. Giulini and A.M.J.J. Bonvin, Combining AI structure prediction and integrative modelling for nanobody-antigen complexes, *J. Chem. Inf. Model.* 66, 11445-11458 (2026).

## Dataset

- **Input list**: `nanobody-antigen-input.txt` — generated by running `setup.sh`
- **Molecule suffixes**: `_af_multi_imbuilder_ensemble` (nanobody ensemble, IBMu: ImmuneBuilder and AlphaFold2-Multimer models), `_l_u` (antigen, unbound)
- **Restraint files**: `_real_ambig.tbl`, `_loose_ambig.tbl`, `_twohit_ambig.tbl`, `_allsurface_ambig.tbl` (ambiguous restraints of the four information scenarios), `_l_u_unambig.tbl` (unambiguous restraints keeping the chains of the unbound antigen together; empty for single-chain antigens)
- **Reference structure**: `_ref.pdb` — used for CAPRI evaluation

The dataset repository also has other nanobody ensembles (IB, IBMo, IBMM), the bound antigen (`_l_b`) and mixed-paratope restraints; they are not used here.

## Scenarios

The workflows follow the example configuration files of the dataset (`cfg_files/`): rigid-body docking (1000 models), selection of the top 200, flexible refinement (`flexref`, tolerance 25), energy minimisation (`emref`), FCC clustering and the top 4 models of each cluster.

### unbound_real-interface

Ambiguous restraints from the real interface (`_real_ambig.tbl`), as defined in the paper.

### unbound_loose-interface

Ambiguous restraints from a loosely defined interface (`_loose_ambig.tbl`), as defined in the paper.

### unbound_twohit-interface

Ambiguous restraints of the two-hit interface scenario (`_twohit_ambig.tbl`), as defined in the paper.

### unbound_all-surface

Ambiguous restraints to the whole surface of the antigen (`_allsurface_ambig.tbl`), as defined in the paper. Without information on the epitope, 10000 rigid-body models are generated and clustered (FCC), and the top 10 models of each cluster are refined.
